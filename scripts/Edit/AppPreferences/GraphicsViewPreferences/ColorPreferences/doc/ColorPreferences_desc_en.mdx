Application preference page for the colors used in the graphics view, such
as background and crosshair colors.

#### Selection

*Primary selection color*

Color in which selected entities are highlighted.

*Highlight selected entities with a halo*

Tick this check box to display selected entities in their own colors with a
blurred halo in the primary selection color behind them. The entity under the
mouse cursor is highlighted with a halo in a lighter or darker shade of its
color. Untick this check box to display selected entities in the primary
selection color instead.

*Halo size (px)*

Width of the halo around a selected entity in pixels.
